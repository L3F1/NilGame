# Prior art and connection policy

[Celinska-Kopczynska and Kopczynski, Portals to Non-Euclidean Geometries,
Bridges 2022](https://archive.bridgesmathart.org/2022/bridges2022-297.pdf)
is direct prior art: immersive portals between Thurston geometries, ray tracing,
and transported camera directions. Do not claim this project invented the idea.
Their construction requires matching intrinsic portal geometry and imposes
extrinsic-curvature conditions. Under those constraints a direct E3/H3 portal
is unavailable; product geometries provide intermediate connections.

NilGame deliberately uses a different policy: radial aperture correspondence
plus an orthogonal tangent map preserving gameplay speed and radius. It does
not claim the finite aperture map is an isometry, its tangent map is the
position map's differential, or the joined space is a smooth physical metric.
Keep that distinction in public explanations and future renderer decisions.
Strictly matched-interface portals would be a separate construction, not a
silent replacement of saved connections.

Related established projects: [Hyperbolica](https://codeparade.itch.io/hyperbolica),
[Curved Spaces](https://www.geometrygames.org/CurvedSpaces/index.html.en), and
[Thurston geometry VR examples](https://3d.wlu.edu/vr/examples/).

Project opportunity is execution: editable connected spaces, usable first-person
interaction and worthwhile authored experiences. Mathematical novelty and market
demand are separate questions. Tests and tool volume do not establish either.
