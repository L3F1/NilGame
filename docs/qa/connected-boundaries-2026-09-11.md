# Portal rim ordering and boundary display

Starting revision c6d4116. Two different causes of purple were mixed together:
normal exhaustion of the bounded chart, and numerical/traversal uncertainty.
Neither is a geometric surface or proof of empty space beyond the chart.

Fixed a query defect: portal scanning used to return unresolved immediately
at an uncertain rim, even when a solid wall lay strictly before it. The GPU
regression failed before the fix with `Hidden aperture rim erased nearer wall`.
The shader now carries that event's earliest affected distance into its existing
ordered uncertainty handling. It accepts only a hit safely before the event;
reaching the event still refuses. No tolerance was reduced.

The browser corpus adds 24 covered-rim rays (eight angles, radial offsets
−.00005/0/+.00005), checked against CPU ownership and an independent plane-hit
distance, plus eight exposed rims that must remain numerical refusals.
An initial blocker face at y=-1.6 coincided with a crate face and exposed a
separate inactive-face/coincident-root refusal. The isolated rim fixture uses
y=-1.55 instead. That degeneracy is NOT repaired or claimed rare by this test.

Reached domain exits now carry a separate diagnostic kind. Normal display uses
dark screen-space checkers, explicitly labeled unavailable extent. Diagnostic
display restores magenta. Other uncertainty stays magenta. Across the ten-view
48,000-ray comparison, every boundary pixel must agree with CPU domain-exit and
region ownership. A framebuffer comparison checks that toggling changes ONLY
boundary pixels, preserving all hits and other failures exactly.

At the entry pose, 1,930 of 4,800 samples reach the chart edge, explaining most
of the purple background. The green disc seen from entry is the portal's view
of the green room, not a depiction of the whole S3 space. No chart extent, ray
range, primitive geometry, collision, or portal mapping was changed.
