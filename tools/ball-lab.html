Scene v1 authoring. Z is up. Solids are authored entities: you stand on the same surfaces the renderer draws, and you see through a portal with the same map that carries you through it. Select a solid and press Carve to cut a hole in it; a carved scene is marched rather than solved, and the readout says so.
