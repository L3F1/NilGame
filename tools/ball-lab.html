BALL LAB / E3

Scene v1 authoring experiment. Z is up. The floor is an authored plane entity: you stand on the same surface the renderer draws.


Play walks a finite-radius probe through the same field the preview draws. WASD moves, mouse looks, Esc stops. With gravity on, Space jumps; with it off you fly, and Space/Shift go up and down. Editing while playing is allowed - the player is pushed clear, or respawns if there is no way out.
