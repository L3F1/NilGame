A flat E3 room opens through a portal into the COMPLETE 3-sphere (curvature radius 8), and a second portal on the far side of the sphere leads back to the flat room. The sphere has no spherical floor and no chart boundary: an unobstructed great circle closes after 2πR ≈ 50.27, unless you cross a portal first.

From the flat spawn, hold W without turning for about ten seconds to enter the sphere, pass its antipode and return through the second portal. Green balls are spherical landmarks; the blue ball is the flat destination. This is a flight fixture, not a finished room.

No gravity. Click the view for real-time flight: WASD, Space/Shift, mouse look; Escape releases the mouse. Buttons take quarter-unit steps. Mouse look stays upright relative to an up direction carried along your flight and through portals, not a world up. Routine contacts remain steerable; unresolved movement or the flat boundary halts the preview. Reset recovers.

Edit this world: change existing objects and portals, add or remove balls, create, reconnect or remove portal pairs, turn an existing portal anchor, undo changes, or save/load your level.

Magenta marks numerical/traversal uncertainty; thin silhouette outlines remain under investigation. Dark checkers mark the end of the flat region, including when viewed through a portal from inside S3.

Higher resolutions sharpen the view and cost more GPU time. The default prioritizes responsiveness.

New ball in the selected region

Enter every value; your current position is never used. In a complete S3 region choose the chart the position is written in; a flat region uses its own coordinates and has no chart. A ball overlapping your body or a spawn is refused.


New portal pair

Choose a region and position for each end. In S3, also choose the chart that defines its coordinates. Set forward and up to perpendicular unit vectors in that coordinate frame; both ends share one opening radius. Create adds the whole pair, and one Undo removes it. Invalid values leave your world unchanged.

Portals are one-sided. You enter an aperture from the side its forward vector points out of, travelling toward −forward; the other face does not transport you. A portal image seen in S3 may be the far end viewed the long way around the sphere, not a physical endpoint near you.

Reconnect existing portals

Choose both ends for each connection, then apply all changed rows together. To swap ends between two pairs, change both rows before applying. Each endpoint can belong to only one pair. Applying another edit or changing the selected object discards unapplied choices in these rows.

Remove a portal pair

Each button removes the named saved connection and both anchors. Unapplied reconnect choices above are ignored.

Removing the last exit can disconnect a region while you remain inside it. Use Undo, load a saved file, or choose a spawn to recover. If Undo would restore an opening through your current position, step aside and try again.
