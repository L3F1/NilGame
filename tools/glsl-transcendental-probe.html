GLSL transcendental accuracy probe

One decision: may a GLSL spherical root envelope use atan and acos at all? The hit-band review measured the requirement - better than 2-10 rad absolute to keep every guarded pixel, 2-8 to keep all but two - without knowing what a backend delivers. This page measures the delivered accuracy.

Every GPU number comes from a WebGL2 fragment shader evaluating the two built-ins in highp float and read back with gl.readPixels. The oracle is binary64 Math.atan2/Math.acos on the same binary32 inputs. Samples are the coefficients the census actually produces at the recorded pose, plus a sweep over phase, amplitude and ratios pressed against 1 where acos is most sensitive.

Run the real driver and SwiftShader sequentially and record the hardware string with each run. A sampled measurement on two backends of one machine is evidence, never a portable guarantee, and admitting a constant from it is the lead's call.
