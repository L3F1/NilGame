Authoring markers

Spawn and objective entities are EDITOR AIDS, not geometry: they are in no distance field, they occlude nothing, and they spend no shader capacity. Each is drawn as a dashed badge over the view at the pixel whose ray points at it, and every one is listed here with its intrinsic distance so it stays reachable when it is behind you or off screen. Visibility has THREE answers, not two: clear when safe advances covered the whole sightline, behind geometry only when a sample proved a solid in the way, and visibility unknown — amber, with a reason — whenever the conservative field could not settle it. Unknown is not a weak "behind": it is the editor declining to claim either. Both disappear while you fly.

Movement

Free flight: Space rises, Shift descends, Q/E roll. No gravity alignment.

WASD moves, Space rises, Shift descends; the mouse looks and Q/E roll. Escape stops; click the view to resume mouse control. Editing resets the player to the validated region spawn. Free flight preserves roll. Walking follows the authored floor and Space jumps. The red horizon is the chart edge, which is where the numbers stop and not a wall. A movement request that ends owing a correction, or that the kernel cannot resolve, STOPS the flight rather than being flown on from: the refusal appears above with the recoveries that are actually sound for it.
