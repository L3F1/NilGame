Whole-sphere S3 flight

Connected portal preview · Spherical editor · Worlds

The complete 3-sphere, curvature radius 8, with eight coloured landmark balls placed around one great circle. There is no edge: every straight line returns after 2πR ≈ 50.27. Rays look along one full circuit, so a landmark can appear beyond πR, on the far side of the sphere. Colours dim with distance travelled along the ray.

No gravity and no collision: you can fly through a landmark. From inside one, the view refuses (magenta). Magenta also marks rays too close to call: grazing edges and ties.

Click the view for flight: WASD, Space/Shift, mouse look; Escape releases the mouse. Mouse look stays upright relative to the orientation carried along your flight. Escape also stops the automatic loop.
